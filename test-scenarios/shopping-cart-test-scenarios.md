
# SauceDemo – Shopping Cart Test Scenarios

**Project:** SauceDemo  
**Module:** Shopping Cart  
**Testing Type:** Functional Testing  
**Website:** https://www.saucedemo.com/

## 1. Positive Test Scenarios

### TS001 – Add a product to the cart
Verify that a user can successfully add a product to the shopping cart.

### TS002 – Remove a product from the cart
Verify that a user can successfully remove a product from the shopping cart.

### TS003 – Verify product name in the cart
Verify that the correct product name is displayed in the shopping cart.

### TS004 – Verify product quantity
Verify that the shopping cart displays the correct product quantity.

### TS005 – Verify cart count
Verify that the cart count updates correctly when products are added or removed.

### TS006 – Continue shopping
Verify that a user can return to the products page from the shopping cart.

### TS007 – Proceed to checkout
Verify that a user can proceed to the checkout process from the shopping cart.

### TS008 – Verify product price displayed in the cart.
Verify product price displayed in the shopping cart.

## 2. Empty-State and Boundary Test Scenarios

### TS009 – Empty shopping cart
Verify that the shopping cart displays an appropriate empty-cart state when no products have been added.
